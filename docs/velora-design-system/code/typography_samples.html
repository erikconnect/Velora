03. Body Variants

Font: Manrope
Standard 16px / 1.6 / 0.01em

Our standard body text is optimized for long-form reading on dark surfaces. The Manrope typeface provides a warmth that balances the mechanical sharpness of our display styles. It ensures high legibility across all screen densities.

Medium 18px / 1.5 / 0em

Medium variants are used for lead paragraphs and introductory content. The increased weight provides a subtle hierarchy shift without requiring a change in typeface or color.

Small 14px / 1.4 / 0.02em

The small body variant is utilized for secondary information, descriptions, and supporting copy. It maintains a tighter leading to optimize space while slightly increasing character tracking for optical clarity at reduced scales.
