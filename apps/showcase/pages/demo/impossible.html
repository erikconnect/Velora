Depth without dimensions.

Layer 1

Perspective transforms

3D perspective creates physical depth. Elements emerge from behind the screen, rotating into view as you scroll. The transform-style: preserve-3d property maintains spatial relationships between layers.

Layer 2

Atmospheric blur

Depth-of-field simulation through animated filter: blur(). Distant elements start blurred and sharpen as they approach the focal plane. A technique borrowed from cinematography, now native to the browser.

Layer 3

Scale transitions

Elements scale from 0.85 to 1.0 as they enter the viewport, simulating physical approach. Combined with opacity and perspective, this creates the illusion of objects moving through three-dimensional space.
