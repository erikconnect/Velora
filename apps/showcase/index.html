Section timeline

Reveal by section.
No scroll scrubbing.

Each block appears when its section enters a defined range. Motion follows content order and reading rhythm instead of continuous scrub.

  1. Intent
  2. Structure
  3. Depth
  4. Conversion
Step 01

Intent appears first

Start each section with a clear, short message. Use early range reveals so users understand context before they hit detail.

Step 02

Structure follows context

Cards, lists, and examples enter in the middle of the viewport journey, after headline and lead copy are already established.

Step 03

Depth appears later

Technical details and advanced controls reveal late, preventing overload and keeping cadence calm and editorial.

Step 04

CTA closes the sequence

Action appears last, after trust and comprehension are built. This keeps conversion tied to narrative completion.
