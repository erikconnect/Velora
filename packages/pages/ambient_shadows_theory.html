Ambient Shadows Theory

A departure from directional lighting. Velora utilizes a multi-layered atmospheric shadow system to simulate depth through occlusion and translucency.

Introduction to Ambient Shadows

Traditional UI shadows often rely on a fixed "Global Light" source, resulting in harsh directional offsets. Velora rejects this in favor of Ambient Shadows.

By centering the shadow around the element and utilizing high blur values, we simulate an environment where components are suspended in a viscous, semi-translucent fluid. Light doesn't just hit the surface; it passes through it, picking up the "Kinetic Green" tints of the underlying architecture.
