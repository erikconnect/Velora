Editorial Brute

We reject the 'Modern Web' template—characterized by soft gradients and rounded corners. The Editorial Brute concept treats typography as the primary graphic element, replacing photography with massive, architectural letterforms.

In this philosophy, the DISPLAY_ULTRA scale serves as the anchor. It creates its own environment of hierarchy, allowing secondary and tertiary information to be read as technical metadata rather than marketing copy.

  • Asymmetric composition as a standard layout principle.
  • High-contrast surfaces that emphasize glyph sharpness.
  • Minimalist color palette focused on Kinetic Green and Surface Noir.
