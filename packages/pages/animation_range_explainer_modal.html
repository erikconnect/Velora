dashboard_customize
Kinetic Card

Entry

Starts when the element enters the viewport. Specifically, when the first edge of the element intersects with the edge of the viewport. Ideal for reveal animations and staggered entrance effects.

Cover

Spans the entire duration the element is visible. This range tracks the element from the moment it first touches the viewport until it has completely exited the opposite side. Perfect for parallax scrolling.
